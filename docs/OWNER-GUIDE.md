# Updating The Collaborative website

Use Sanity Studio to update the website’s **Gallery photos** and **homepage Announcements**. You can do this in your web browser without writing code or installing anything.

**Open Studio: [collaborative-ajj.sanity.studio](https://collaborative-ajj.sanity.studio/)**

Bookmark this link so it is easy to find next time.

## Sign in

1. Open the Studio link above.
2. Sign in with the account that was invited to manage the website. If you received an invitation email, accept it first.
3. Under **Website content**, choose **Gallery** or **Announcements**.

If you cannot get in, ask the person who manages the website to invite your email address or check your editing access.

## The most important step: Publish

Studio saves your edits as a **draft** while you work. A draft is a work in progress; it does not change what visitors see on the website. When you are ready, click **Publish** to make your changes available on the site.

When editing something that is already published, visitors keep seeing the previous published version until you publish again. Wait for Studio to finish saving before closing your browser.

After publishing, open the website and check your work. Updates are not instant: wait a minute or two, then reload the page. You may need to reload again after a short wait. You do not need to rebuild the website or ask someone to upload your changes.

## Add photos to the Gallery

1. Choose **Gallery**.
2. In **Photos**, use the add control to add a photo, then upload an image from your device. You can also select an image that has already been uploaded.
3. Open the photo’s details and fill in **Image description**. This is required. Describe what is visible so someone using a screen reader can understand the photo. For example: “Students practicing a takedown with their coach on the mats.”
4. Repeat for any other photos you want to add.
5. Drag the photos into the order you want them to appear.
6. Click **Publish**, then check the website’s **Gallery** page after allowing time for it to update.

Image descriptions are for accessibility; they do not appear as captions underneath the photos.

### Replace a photo or adjust how it fits

Open the photo in Studio and use its image controls to replace the image or adjust its crop. Check the **Image description** whenever you replace a photo so it still matches.

The crop controls let you trim the edges. The **hotspot** marks the important part of the image, such as someone’s face, so the website can keep that area in view when fitting the photo into its space.

The first and fourth gallery positions are taller than the others. After changing the order or crop, publish and check the gallery on both your phone and computer.

### Remove a photo

1. Find the photo in the Gallery’s **Photos** list.
2. Open that item’s menu and choose the option to remove it from the list.
3. Click **Publish**.

Removing a photo from this list removes it from the website gallery after the site updates. It does not change photos used on other website pages.

If you remove every photo and publish, the gallery will display “New photos are on the way. Check back soon.” To bring photos back, add them and publish again.

## Post an announcement

Announcements appear on the homepage. Use them for updates such as holiday hours, seminars, or gym news.

1. Choose **Announcements**.
2. Use the create/new control in the announcement list to start an **Announcement**.
3. Fill in the fields below.
4. Read through your message and check the dates.
5. Click **Publish**, then check the homepage after allowing time for it to update.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | A short heading, such as “Holiday hours this Monday.” Required. |
| **Message** | The details visitors need. Use normal text; line breaks are supported. Formatting such as bold text or HTML is not supported. Required. |
| **Announcement date** | The date displayed with the post. It also controls the order: newer dates appear first. Required. |
| **Show through** | Optional: the last day you want the post to appear, using California time. Leave it blank if you do not want it to expire automatically. |

**Example:** For a Saturday seminar, use the date you are sharing the news as the **Announcement date**, and the seminar’s date as **Show through**. Put the seminar’s time and other details in the **Message**.

### How dates and visibility work

- The homepage shows **up to three published, non-expired announcements**, with the newest announcement dates first. Older posts remain in Studio even when they are no longer among those three.
- **Announcement date does not schedule a post.** A future-dated post can appear as soon as you publish it. To share something later, leave it as a draft and return to publish it when ready.
- **Show through** keeps a post active through the end of that day in California. It disappears after the website next updates successfully, so removal may not happen at exactly midnight.
- An expired post stays in Studio. To use it again, edit its message and dates, clear or extend **Show through**, and publish. It still needs to be among the three newest active posts to appear.

### Edit or take down an announcement

To **edit**, open **Announcements**, select the post, make your changes, and click **Publish** again.

To **take it off the website but keep it for later**, open the post’s document actions menu near the publishing controls and choose **Unpublish**. Allow time for the website to update. You can publish the post again later.

To **remove the post entirely**, choose **Delete** from its document actions menu and follow the confirmation prompt. If you only want to hide it for now, use **Unpublish**.

## Before you finish

- Check names, times, dates, and spelling.
- Make sure each photo has an accurate **Image description**.
- Click **Publish** for the Gallery or each announcement you changed. Publishing one does not publish the others.
- Wait for the website to update, reload it, and check the result on your phone.

## If something does not look right

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| My change is saved in Studio but missing from the website. | Check that you clicked **Publish**. Wait a minute or two and reload the website; try another reload after a short wait. |
| My announcement still does not appear. | Check whether **Show through** is in the past, and whether three other active posts have newer **Announcement dates**. |
| I cannot publish. | Look for an error beside a field. Photos need an **Image description**; announcements need a **Title**, **Message**, and **Announcement date**. Wait for uploads to finish. If editing or publishing controls are unavailable, ask the website manager to check your access. |
| A face or other important detail is cut off. | Adjust the photo’s crop and hotspot, publish, and check the gallery again. |
| I published a typo or incorrect detail. | Open the item, correct it, and publish again. |
| Changes still do not appear after several minutes and repeated reloads. | Contact the website manager with the item you edited and what you expected to see. |

## Other website changes

Studio currently controls only the **Gallery** and **Announcements**. For changes to the class schedule, instructor information, About page, contact details, other page text or photos, or the site’s design, contact the person who manages the website.
